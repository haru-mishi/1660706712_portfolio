            PRJ-05
            shipped
          

          
A FastAPI + Postgres backend slice proving concurrent orders can't oversell shared stock: a SELECT ... FOR UPDATE row lock serializes contention on the order endpoint, so 30 simultaneous requests against 10 units of stock resolve to exactly 10 accepted and 20 cleanly rejected — never negative, never double-sold. Wrapped in a small register-style demo UI, with the stress test tucked behind a hidden control since the lock is the point, not the ordering flow.
